## Overview
Shadow Ninja ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

## 1. Data Collection & Usage
**Shadow Ninja collects zero personal data.** 
- We do not collect, store, track, or share any personal information, device identifiers, location data, or usage analytics.
- The app operates entirely offline and does not require or request an internet connection permission (`INTERNET` permission is absent).

## 2. Local Storage & Save Data
All game progress, level completion status, unlocked skills, settings, and coin counts are saved locally on your device in a secure sandbox file (`user://shadow_ninja_save.json`, plus settings in `user://shadow_ninja_settings.cfg`). 
- This data never leaves your device.
- Uninstalling the application may remove local save data.

## 3. Third-Party Services & Ads
- Shadow Ninja contains **no third-party ads**, tracking SDKs, or analytics tools.
- There are no in-app purchases (IAP) or external payment gateways.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Because we collect no personal data whatsoever, we expect no COPPA or GDPR-K data obligations. This is a draft and is not legal advice; review it before publishing.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document with an updated effective date.
