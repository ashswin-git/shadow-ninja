Privacy Policy for Shadow Ninja

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

Shadow Ninja ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our game handles your information.

1. Data Collection

Shadow Ninja does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, user information, or telemetry. We do not require account creation, registration, or login.

2. Ads and Analytics

The game contains no third-party advertisements, tracking tools, or analytics SDKs.

3. Local Storage

All game progress, scores, and settings are saved locally on your device storage. We do not have access to this data.

4. Internet Access

Shadow Ninja is designed to work completely offline and does not require an active internet connection to play.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/ashswin-git/shadow-ninja/issues.
