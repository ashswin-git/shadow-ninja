# UI Checklist & Design Audit — Aether Shinobi: Spirit Blade

## Design System & Theme
- **Theme**: Anime action platformer inspired by cursed energy combat, deep indigo nights (`#120d26`), cursed blue (`#3b82f6`) and violet (`#8b5cf6`) energy effects, and spirit red enemies (`#ef4444`).
- **Typography**: Open Sauce Sans (Regular & Bold) loaded via ContentPack.
- **Color Contrast**: AAA compliant contrast across HUD, menus, and pause screens.

## Screens & Components
1. **Title / Main Menu**:
   - Dynamic background with glowing moon and floating energy particles.
   - Clean buttons for Play, Level Select, Settings, and Quit.
2. **Level Select**:
   - Grid/list of unlocked levels with star ratings and thumbnail previews.
3. **Settings**:
   - Music & SFX volume sliders, touch controls toggle, vibration switch.
4. **HUD**:
   - HP hearts/bar, ultimate energy meter (charges with hits/coins, triggers screen-wide domain freeze), score / coin counter, current level label.
5. **Touch Controls**:
   - Big translucent mobile controls (>= 110px at 720p) positioned on left (movement) and right (jump, attack, dash, ultimate).
6. **Pause, Victory & Defeat Menus**:
   - Frosted glass overlay, clear navigation options (Resume, Restart, Main Menu).
