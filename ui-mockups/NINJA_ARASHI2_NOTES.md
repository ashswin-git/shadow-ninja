# Ninja Arashi 2 – reference notes (feel to match, assets NOT copied)
Source: store screenshots in reference/ninja_arashi2/ (copies of 4 in ui-mockups/reference/). Play Store page was not fetchable from the box (404/blocked), so notes come from the screenshots plus general knowledge of the game.

## Art style
- Pure-black (#000–#0b0d0e) silhouette foreground: ground, rocks, grass tufts, hanging moss, ninja, enemies. No outlines or interior detail; readability comes from shape alone.
- Backgrounds are 2–4 desaturated layers: teal/cyan forest fog (#2f6f6e → #9cc6be), pale grey-green mist, and blue-grey mountains. Each layer is lighter and less saturated with distance (atmospheric perspective).
- The only saturated colour is RED (#c4161c → #ff2a2a), reserved for danger: spikes, saw blades, spiked balls, blood and enemy eyes. Cyan/white is used for interactables: water, energy, portals and checkpoint glows.
- Organic edges: jagged grass on platform tops, hanging vines, broken wooden structures and chains.
- Fog bands and light shafts between layers; floating dust and leaves.

## Animation
- Ninja: idle breathing with a scarf flutter, a fast run (about 8–10 frames), a jump with a squash on takeoff and land, a mid-air flip on double jump, a dash smear, wall-slide with dust, wall-jump kick-off, a sword-slash arc (white or red smear, 3–4 frames), shuriken throw, and a hit flash then a ragdoll-like death.
- Enemies: patrol walk, an alert "!", melee wind-up telegraph, archers' draw and release, and a death burst of black particles plus red spray.

## HUD layout
- Top-left: health hearts/bar and a coin or diamond count. Top-right: a pause button. Very minimal and semi-transparent; it never covers the play space.
- Bottom-left: a left/right arrow pair. Bottom-right: a cluster of a big JUMP button, plus ATTACK, SHURIKEN and DASH/skill buttons with cooldown rings. Buttons are translucent white/grey circles about 110–140 px at 720p.

## Traps
Floor and wall spikes, spinning saws on rails, swinging spiked balls on chains, crushers, falling platforms, rotating spiked gears, arrows, water pits and lasers/energy beams.

## Enemies
Spear/sword guards, archers, ranged throwers, shielded guards, beasts, and bosses with telegraphed big attacks.

## Game feel
Tight, responsive controls with high gravity and quick fall, coyote time, jump buffering, variable jump height, screen shake on hits and deaths, hit-stop, particles everywhere (dust, sparks, blood), checkpoint saves and short levels with instant respawn.

## Our original direction
"Shadow Ninja" keeps the black-silhouette-over-fog approach but uses its own palette (deep teal-jade night fog with a large pale moon and red-lacquer accents), its own bamboo/temple motifs, a brush-script title and a pixel UI font. All art is original or CC0.
